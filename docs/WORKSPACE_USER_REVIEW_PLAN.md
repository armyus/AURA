# AURA workspace review plan

## Goal

Check whether five students or early-career professionals can complete a decision comparison without assistance, understand why scores differ, and identify the next useful fact to verify.

## 20-minute session

1. Ask the participant to compare two plausible opportunities using a supplied fictional scenario.
2. Ask them to create two alternatives and at least three criteria, then set ratings and one hard constraint.
3. Ask them to find a missing fact in a supplied source excerpt, verify it, and update the model.
4. Ask them to run a baseline and change one assumption in the scenario lab.
5. Ask them to explain what the result does and does not mean and where they would go next.

## Record

- Completion without moderator help (yes/no) and elapsed time.
- Steps where the participant hesitates, makes an error, or misunderstands a label.
- Whether the participant can identify the input, weight, constraint, and source behind a displayed result.
- Whether the participant interprets scores or simulation lead shares as a real-world probability.
- Post-task usefulness and trust ratings from 1 (low) to 5 (high), with one open-ended reason.

## Exit criteria from the roadmap

The workspace passes when all five participants complete a modeled decision without AI, the keyboard-only path has no blocking defect, and score/uncertainty caveats are understood. Report actual observations and participant count; do not infer user validation from internal testing.
